How Adoption Works

Adoption is a commitment. Take your time and prepare to give your future companion a safe and caring home.

1

Find Your Match

Browse the available cat profiles and use the search and filters to find a cat that fits your preferences.

2

View Cat Details

Click View Details to learn more about your chosen cat, including its description, health information, and shelter contact details.

3

Contact the Shelter

Contact the shelter using the information provided to confirm that the cat is still available and ask if you can reserve it. Confirm the adoption requirements and arrange your visit before going to the shelter.

Every Paw Deserves a Place to Belong.

Purrfect Home is a platform where animal shelters can showcase cats available for adoption. It helps people browse cat profiles, learn about their personalities and needs, and prepare before visiting the respective shelter. By viewing the available listings in advance, potential adopters can make more informed decisions and contact the shelter for further details.

Want to List a Cat for Adoption?

Are you an animal shelter, rescuer, or someone who can no longer care for a cat? You can contact the website administrator to inquire about featuring your cat on Purrfect Home and helping it find a loving home.
