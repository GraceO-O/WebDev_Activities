How adoption works

Adoption is a commitment. Take your time and make sure you and your future companion are ready for each other.

1

Get to know them

Browse the cat profiles and read about each cat's age, personality, and needs.

2

Prepare your home

Plan for food, litter, a safe resting place, veterinary care, and time together.

3

Ask about adoption

Contact a real shelter or rescue organization to learn about its application and adoption process.
